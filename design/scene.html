Live Battlefield戦場

Everything assembled and moving: painted ground, generated tower and enemy sprites, procedural path, magic-circle decals, projectiles, impact bursts, damage numbers and the crystal leak ring. This is the T16 render layer as a working reference, not a mockup.

It also implements the T31 loop exactly as specified — fixed 16.67ms steps, a 250ms rawDt clamp, a step budget that scales with speed so fast-forward does not degrade into slow motion, and render interpolation via alpha = acc / DT. Change speed and watch the step counter: it tracks speed × elapsed × 60, and the dt handed to step() never changes.
