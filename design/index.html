React TD — Design ReferencesリアクトTD ・ 魔法の塔防衛

Hand-built HTML, not AI-generated mockups. Every screen encodes the decisions from the /autoplan review: dark navy chrome with gold hairlines (not cream), English-first labels with kanji as flavour only, Fire / Ice / Lightning / Wind, a three-segment leak ring rather than a crystal HP bar, and the tower panel swapping into the card rail instead of opening a modal. Portraits are real crops from references/ via design/atlas/.

Frames are 375×812. The stated floor is 360×640 — narrow your window to check the rail still holds four 44px cards. Fonts here are system fallbacks so this renders offline; DESIGN.md (task T0) must name the real faces.
